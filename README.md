# 📊 Sales Performance & Profitability Analysis (Excel)

An Excel-based analysis of a retail sales dataset (January 2014 – December 2017, approximately 9,000+ sales records) to understand sales trends, identify the most profitable categories, evaluate customer growth and uncover areas where the business is losing profit.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

The goal was to turn raw retail sales data into an interactive dashboard that answers business questions, highlights profitability trends and provides actionable recommendations.

**Dataset:** 9,000+ sales records covering January 2014 to December 2017, with fields such as order date, customer name, state, category, sub-category, product name, sales, quantity and profit.

---

## 🤔 Business Questions

### ❓ 1. How has profit changed over time, and which categories drive it?

Total profit increased by 88.6%, from about $49.6K in 2014 to $93.4K in 2017, growing every year. Technology contributed the highest total profit ($145.5K), followed by Office Supplies ($122.5K). Furniture contributed only $18.5K despite generating substantial sales.

### ❓ 2. Which sub-categories contribute the most to total sales?

Phones (14.4%) and Chairs (14.3%) together account for nearly 29% of total sales. Storage (9.7%), Tables (9.0%) and Binders (8.9%) follow. These five sub-categories contribute about 51% of total sales.

### ❓ 3. Which states generate the most sales?

California leads with about $457.7K (20% of total sales), followed by New York ($310.9K), Texas ($170.2K), Washington ($138.6K) and Pennsylvania ($116.5K). California and New York together contribute about 33.5% of total sales.

### ❓ 4. Who are the top 5 customers by profit?

The top five customers are Tamara Chand ($8.98K), Raymond Buch ($6.98K), Sanjit Chand ($5.76K), Hunter Lopez ($5.62K) and Adrian Barton ($5.44K). Together, they contribute approximately $32.8K, or 11.4% of total profit.

### ❓ 5. Is the customer base growing year over year?

Yes, with a dip in 2015. The number of active customers went from 595 in 2014 to 573 in 2015, then increased to 638 in 2016 and 693 in 2017, representing approximately 16.5% growth between 2014 and 2017.

### ❓ 6. Are there seasonal patterns in monthly sales?

Yes. Sales are strongest towards the end of the year, with November ($352.5K), December ($325.3K) and September ($307.6K) together contributing about 42.9% of total sales. February is the weakest month, with approximately $59.8K in sales.

---

## 📈 Headline Numbers

- **Total Sales:** Approximately $2.30M
- **Total Profit:** Approximately $286.4K
- **Profit Growth:** 88.6% (2014–2017)
- **Top Sales States:** California, New York, Texas, Washington and Pennsylvania
- **Top Sales Sub-Categories:** Phones and Chairs
- **Highest Profit Categories:** Technology and Office Supplies

---

## 💡 Recommendations

1. Prioritise Technology and Office Supplies, as they generate the majority of total profit.
2. Review pricing, discounting and costs in Furniture.
3. Focus inventory and marketing efforts on high-selling sub-categories such as Phones and Chairs.
4. Plan inventory and promotional campaigns around the November–December sales peak, while using targeted offers to improve weaker months such as February.
5. Build loyalty programmes and retention strategies for high-profit customers.
6. Strengthen sales in leading markets such as California and New York while identifying opportunities in other states.
